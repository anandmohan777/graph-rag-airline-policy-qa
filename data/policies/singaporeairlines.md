# SINGAPORE AIRLINES BOOKING POLICY DOCUMENT
## Revised Structure for GraphRAG Dissertation Testing

---

## **SIA1: General Booking Terms**

All SingaporeAirlines bookings are subject to fare rules at time of purchase. Tickets are valid for the specific route, date, and cabin class booked. Fare types determine eligibility for changes, cancellations, and refunds. Infants under 2 years travel without a seat assignment. All policies comply with Singapore Civil Aviation Authority regulations and international air transport agreements. Tickets are non-transferable and must match the passenger's government-issued identification.

---

## **SIA2: Economy Lite Fare**

Economy Lite is the most restrictive non-refundable fare type. No refund is provided for voluntary cancellations after the 24-hour booking window. Changes are not permitted except within the 24-hour period. Seat selection requires payment for all seat types. Passengers receive standard 30kg checked baggage allowance and earn 50% KrisFlyer miles. Visa denial and natural disaster exceptions may apply with proper documentation.

**References:** See SIA5 for 24-hour cancellation window, SIA12 for visa denial refunds, SIA19 for natural disaster exceptions

---

## **SIA3: Economy Standard Fare**

Economy Standard allows flight changes with applicable fees. Cancellations are permitted but non-refundable except for visa denial and natural disaster cases. Standard seats can be selected in advance for free. Forward Zone and Extra Legroom seats require payment. Passengers receive 30kg baggage allowance and earn 75% KrisFlyer miles. Partial journey cancellations are allowed for multi-segment bookings.

**References:** See SIA5 for 24-hour exceptions, SIA14 for standard change fees, SIA19 for natural disaster exceptions, SIA23 for partial segment cancellation

---

## **SIA4: Economy Flexi Fare**

Economy Flexi is the most flexible economy option. Flight changes are permitted with reduced fees. Cancellations receive partial refund based on timing. Standard and Forward Zone seats are free to select in advance. Passengers receive 35kg baggage allowance and earn 100% KrisFlyer miles. Visa denial and natural disasters qualify for full refunds. Same-day standby is allowed subject to availability.

**References:** See SIA5 for 24-hour full refund guarantee, SIA12 for visa denial refunds, SIA19 for natural disaster exceptions

---

## **SIA5: 24-Hour Cancellation Guarantee**

All tickets booked directly through singaporeair.com or SingaporeAirlines reservations can be cancelled for full refund within 24 hours of purchase. The booking must be made at least 7 days before scheduled departure. Refund processes automatically to the original payment method within 5-7 working days. This applies to all fare types including Economy Lite. Third-party agency bookings are not eligible for this guarantee.

---

## **SIA6: Premium Economy Standard Fare**

Premium Economy Standard offers enhanced comfort with wider seats and increased legroom. Changes allowed with applicable fees. Cancellations are non-refundable except for visa denial and natural disasters. Standard seats free, Extra Legroom seats require payment. Passengers receive 35kg baggage allowance and earn 100% KrisFlyer miles. Not eligible for mileage upgrades to Business Class. Involuntary cabin downgrades receive compensation.

**References:** See SIA5 for 24-hour cancellation, SIA14 for change fees, SIA22 for cabin downgrade compensation

---

## **SIA7: Premium Economy Flexi Fare**

Premium Economy Flexi provides maximum flexibility with reduced change fees. Partial refunds available on cancellations based on timing. All Premium Economy seats including Extra Legroom can be selected free. Passengers receive 35kg baggage allowance and earn 125% KrisFlyer miles. Eligible for mileage upgrades to Business Class at reduced rates. Involuntary downgrades compensated according to EU regulations.

**References:** See SIA5 for 24-hour guarantee, SIA22 for cabin downgrade compensation

---

## **SIA8: Business Lite Fare**

Business Lite offers Business Class service at a reduced price with restrictions. Changes permitted with fees based on timing and route. Cancellations are non-refundable except for visa denial and natural disasters. Passengers receive 40kg baggage allowance, lounge access, and earn 125% KrisFlyer miles. Priority boarding and premium meal service included. All Business Class seats can be selected free in advance.

**References:** See SIA5 for 24-hour window, SIA14 for change fees, SIA15 for route-based penalties

---

## **SIA9: Business Standard Fare**

Business Standard allows flexible changes with moderate fees. Cancellations receive partial refund based on timing before departure. Passengers receive 40kg baggage allowance, lounge access, and earn 150% KrisFlyer miles. Priority check-in and boarding included. Involuntary schedule changes qualify for full refund or free rebooking. All Business Class seats free to select.

**References:** See SIA5 for 24-hour full refund, SIA18 for involuntary schedule changes

---

## **SIA10: Business Flexi & Suites/First Class Fare**

Business Flexi and all First Class/Suites tickets offer maximum flexibility. Changes allowed anytime before departure with minimal or no fees. Cancellations receive substantial refund regardless of timing. Passengers receive 50kg baggage allowance (First/Suites), premium lounge access including Private Room, and earn 150% KrisFlyer miles. Complimentary chauffeur service on select routes. Involuntary changes receive priority rebooking.

**References:** See SIA5 for 24-hour guarantee, SIA18 for schedule change policies

---

## **SIA11: Flight Changes & Amendment Fees**

Flight changes are permitted based on fare type and ticket conditions. Changes must be requested before original scheduled departure. Same-day changes allowed up to 2 hours before departure with additional fees. Fare difference applies if new flight is higher priced. Route changes to different destinations incur higher fees. Name spellings can be corrected with restrictions.

**References:** See SIA14 for timing-based fees, SIA15 for route-based penalties, SIA26 for name correction policies

---

## **SIA12: Visa Denial & Immigration Document Issues**

Passengers denied entry visa by destination country can request refund or travel credit. Refund eligibility depends on fare type purchased. Economy Lite receives travel voucher only. Economy Standard, Economy Flexi, and all Premium Economy receive full refund minus £45 processing fee. All Business Class fares receive full refund with no processing fee. Visa denial must be for initial application, not visa violations or criminal issues.

**References:** See SIA13 for required documentation

---

## **SIA13: Visa Denial Documentation Requirements**

Required documents include official visa denial letter from embassy or consulate on letterhead. Copy of visa application with application reference number. Proof of scheduled travel dates matching the denied visa dates. Documentation must be submitted within 21 days of visa denial. Submit via email to visarefunds@singaporeair.com or through online portal. Late submissions may be rejected. False documentation results in forfeiture of all refund rights.

---

## **SIA14: Timing-Based Flight Change Charges**

Change fees vary based on days before departure when the change is requested. Fees apply per passenger per flight segment.

- £50 per segment for more than 21 days before departure
- £100 per segment for 15 to 20 days before departure  
- £150 per segment for 8 to 14 days before departure
- £200 per segment for 7 days or less before departure

Same-day changes within 24 hours of departure add £75 per segment. All fees are cumulative with route-based penalties.

**References:** See SIA15 for additional route-based levies

---

## **SIA15: Route-Based Change Penalties**

Long-haul routes exceeding 7 hours flying time add £60 route penalty per direction. Medium-haul routes of 4-7 hours add £40 route penalty. Short-haul routes under 4 hours add £25 route penalty. Penalties apply to changes only, not initial bookings. Route penalties combine with timing-based fees for total change cost. Penalties are waived for involuntary changes by SingaporeAirlines.

---

## **SIA16: SingaporeAirlines Flight Cancellations**

If SingaporeAirlines cancels a flight for operational reasons, passengers receive full refund of all charges including taxes and fees. Alternative flights on SingaporeAirlines or partner carriers offered at no cost. Passengers may also claim compensation under applicable regulations. Refunds process within 7 working days. Cancelled flights due to weather or air traffic restrictions considered extraordinary circumstances.

**References:** See SIA17 for delay-related policies, SIA20 for EC261 compensation eligibility

---

## **SIA17: Flight Delays & Passenger Rights**

Delays exceeding 4 hours entitle passengers to meal vouchers and refreshments. Delays exceeding 6 hours allow passengers to request full refund or rebooking. Overnight delays provide hotel accommodation and ground transportation. Delays under 3 hours do not qualify for compensation. Weather, air traffic control, and security threats are extraordinary circumstances. Delay compensation follows EU261 rules for European departures.

**References:** See SIA20 for EC261 compensation amounts

---

## **SIA18: Involuntary Schedule Changes**

If SingaporeAirlines changes flight schedule by more than 120 minutes, passengers may request full refund or free alternative flight. Last-minute changes within 21 days may qualify for statutory compensation. Alternative routing must be comparable in journey time and cabin class. Multi-segment bookings allow partial refunds for affected segments only.

**References:** See SIA20 for EU261 compensation eligibility

---

## **SIA19: Natural Disaster & Force Majeure Exceptions**

Natural disasters including hurricanes, earthquakes, volcanic eruptions, or severe weather at origin or destination allow cancellations with full refund or travel voucher. Civil unrest, war, or terrorism at destination qualify for refunds. Government-issued travel bans or warnings qualify for voucher or refund. Pandemic-related travel restrictions follow separate government policies. Travel vouchers valid for 24 months from original travel date.

**References:** See SIA21 for travel voucher terms and conditions

---

## **SIA20: EC261 Compensation Eligibility**

If SingaporeAirlines cancels a flight EU Regulation 261/2004 applies to flights departing from EU airports or arriving in EU on SingaporeAirlines. Compensation amounts based on flight distance. Flights under 1,500km qualify for £250 per passenger. Flights 1,500-3,500km qualify for £400 per passenger. Flights over 3,500km qualify for £600 per passenger. Compensation reduced by 50% if arrival delay is under specific thresholds. Not payable for extraordinary circumstances.

---

## **SIA21: Travel Vouchers & Credits**

Travel vouchers issued for visa denials, natural disasters, and some schedule changes. Vouchers valid for 24 months from issue date. Can be used for any SingaporeAirlines or SilkAir flight. Vouchers are non-transferable and must be used by original passenger. Partial use allowed with remaining balance carried forward. Expired vouchers are not extended or refunded. Vouchers have no cash value and cannot be sold.

---

## **SIA22: Involuntary Cabin Downgrade Compensation**

If SingaporeAirlines downgrades passenger to lower cabin class, compensation is provided based on flight distance. Short-haul flights under 1,500km receive 30% ticket refund. Medium-haul flights 1,500-3,500km receive 50% ticket refund. Long-haul flights over 3,500km receive 75% ticket refund. Voluntary downgrades for operational reasons may offer vouchers instead. Refunds process automatically within 14 days of travel.

---

## **SIA23: Partial Segment Cancellations**

Multi-city and multi-segment bookings allow cancellation of individual flight legs. Remaining segments stay valid for travel. Cancellation fees apply separately to each cancelled segment. Refund calculated as difference between original fare and revised journey cost. Partial cancellations must be requested at least 48 hours before affected segment departure. Frequent flyer miles adjusted proportionally for cancelled segments.

**References:** See SIA14 for per-segment cancellation fees, SIA15 for route-specific penalties

---

## **SIA24: Refund Processing Timeline**

Refunds to original credit or debit cards process within 7 working days after approval. Refunds to travel agent accounts process within 10 working days. Cash refunds via bank transfer require up to 30 working days. Visa denial refunds take up to 28 days pending documentation review. Travel vouchers issue within 48 hours electronically. KrisFlyer miles refunds to account within 5 working days.

---

## **SIA25: KrisFlyer Miles Redemption Bookings**

Award tickets booked with KrisFlyer miles follow modified cancellation policies. Cancelled Saver awards refund miles with £75 cancellation fee deducted from taxes. Cancelled Advantage awards refund miles with £50 cancellation fee. Taxes and carrier charges refundable based on fare rules. Miles typically refund to account within 5-7 days. Partially flown award tickets cannot be cancelled, only changed with applicable fees.

**References:** See SIA5 for 24-hour cancellation window applying to award tickets

---

## **SIA26: Name Corrections & Passenger Changes**

Minor spelling errors up to 3 characters can be corrected without fee if requested at least 72 hours before departure. Full name changes to transfer ticket to another person are not permitted. Name corrections within 72 hours of departure incur £50 administrative fee. Legal name changes due to marriage or divorce require government documents. If name correction is denied or passenger name change needed, ticket must be cancelled and rebooked with fees applying.

**References:** See SIA14 for applicable cancellation and rebooking fees if required

---

## **SIA27: Involuntary Denied Boarding**

If SingaporeAirlines denies boarding due to overbooking, passengers entitled to compensation under EU261 for European departures. Alternative flight must be offered within 3 hours or full refund provided. Meal vouchers, hotel accommodation, and ground transport provided as needed. Denied boarding compensation is additional to refund options. Voluntary denied boarding offers provide travel vouchers or KrisFlyer miles in exchange for taking later flight.

**References:** See SIA20 for EC261 compensation amounts

---

## **SIA28: Wheelchair & Mobility Assistance Passengers**

Passengers requiring wheelchairs or mobility devices receive free assistance throughout airport and flight. Wheelchairs and mobility aids do not count toward baggage allowance. Pre-notification required at least 48 hours before departure. Battery-powered mobility devices must meet aviation safety standards. SingaporeAirlines provides aisle chairs on all aircraft. Companion travelers may be required for passengers needing extensive assistance.

---

## **SIA29: Pregnancy Travel Restrictions**

Pregnant passengers can travel up to end of 36th week for single pregnancy. Twin or multiple pregnancy passengers can travel up to end of 32nd week. Medical certificate required after 28 weeks of pregnancy. Certificate must be dated within 7 days of departure and state expected delivery date and fitness to fly. Travel not recommended in final 4 weeks of pregnancy. SingaporeAirlines may deny boarding without valid medical certificate.

---

## **SIA30: Unaccompanied Minor Policy**

Children aged 5-11 years traveling alone must use Unaccompanied Minor service. Service fee of £75 per child per direction applies. Children are supervised from check-in until collection by authorized adult. Service includes escort through airport, priority boarding, and in-flight monitoring. Parent or guardian must remain at airport until flight departs. Collecting adult must provide government-issued identification matching booking details. Service available on direct flights only.

---

## **SIA31: Group Booking Cancellations**

Group bookings of 10 or more passengers follow specialized terms. Partial group cancellations allowed up to 20% of total passengers without penalty if made 60+ days before departure. Full group cancellations incur negotiated fees based on booking contract. Individual passengers within groups may cancel for visa denial or natural disasters with standard documentation. Group refunds process within 30 working days of approval.

**References:** See SIA12 for visa denial exception rules, SIA19 for natural disaster group exceptions

---
