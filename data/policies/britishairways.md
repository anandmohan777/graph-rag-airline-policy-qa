
# BRITISH AIRWAYS BOOKING POLICY DOCUMENT
## Structure for GraphRAG Dissertation Testing

---

## **BA1: General Booking Terms**

All British Airways bookings are subject to fare rules at time of purchase. Tickets are valid for the specific route, date, and cabin class booked. Fare types determine eligibility for changes, cancellations, and refunds. Children under 2 travel as infants without a seat assignment. All policies comply with UK Civil Aviation Authority regulations and applicable consumer protection laws.

---

## **BA2: Economy Basic Fare**

Economy Basic is a non-refundable fare type. No refund is provided for voluntary cancellations after the 24-hour grace period. Tickets cancelled after departure are not eligible for any residual value credit. Medical emergencies and immediate family bereavement are the only exceptions to the non-refundable policy.

**References:** See BA5 for 24-hour exceptions, BA11 for medical cancellations, BA18 for bereavement cancellations

---

## **BA3: Economy Flexible Fare**

Economy Flexible allows voluntary cancellations with applicable fees. Cancellation charges depend on timing before departure and route category. Partial journey cancellations are permitted for multi-leg bookings. Fee waivers apply for medical emergencies and immediate family bereavement with proper documentation.

**References:** See BA5 for 24-hour exceptions, BA11 for medical cancellations, BA14 for standard cancellation fees, BA18 for bereavement cancellations, BA22 for partial journey rules

---

## **BA4: Business Flexible Fare**

Business Flexible tickets can be cancelled anytime before departure. A flat administrative fee of £25 applies to all voluntary cancellations. Refund is processed to original payment method within 7 working days. No additional documentation required for standard cancellations. Medical and bereavement exemptions follow the same rules as Economy Flexible.

**References:** See BA11 for medical fee waivers, BA18 for bereavement fee waivers

---

## **BA5: 24-Hour Cancellation Guarantee**

All tickets booked directly through British Airways can be cancelled for full refund within 24 hours of purchase. The booking must be made at least 7 days before scheduled departure. Refund is automatic with no documentation required. This applies to all fare types including Economy Basic. Third-party bookings through agents are not eligible.

---

## **BA6: Special Assistance - Medical Equipment**

Passengers requiring medical equipment may bring mobility aids and oxygen concentrators free of charge. Pre-notification is required at least 48 hours before departure. Medical certificates must confirm equipment is battery-powered and flight-safe. Equipment does not count toward standard baggage allowance.

---

## **BA7: In-Flight Medical Services**

British Airways aircraft carry emergency medical kits and automated external defibrillators. Cabin crew are trained in first aid and emergency response. Passengers with specific medical conditions should carry personal medication in hand luggage. In-flight medical emergencies may result in diversions to nearest suitable airport.

---

## **BA8: Travel Insurance - Medical Coverage**

British Airways recommends comprehensive travel insurance covering medical emergencies, trip cancellations, and lost baggage. Insurance must be purchased before departure. Medical claims for trip interruption should be filed with insurance provider. Insurance does not replace BA cancellation policies but provides additional coverage.

---

## **BA9: Flight Changes & Amendment Fees**

Flight changes are permitted based on fare type and ticket conditions. Change fees range from £35 to £125 depending on route and advance notice. Fare difference applies if new flight is higher priced. Changes made within 24 hours of departure incur same-day change fees. Name corrections follow separate procedures.

**References:** See BA25 for name correction policies

---

## **BA10: Compassionate Travel - Bereavement Booking Discounts**

British Airways offers discounted bereavement fares for immediate family deaths requiring urgent travel. Discount applies to new bookings only, not existing reservations. Death certificate or funeral notice required within 48 hours of booking. Bereavement fares are non-refundable but allow free date changes.

---

## **BA11: Medical Emergency Eligibility & Refund Terms**

Medical cancellations require passenger or immediate family member hospitalization or serious medical diagnosis. Refund amount depends on fare type purchased. Economy Basic receives full refund minus £50 processing fee. Economy Flexible and Business Flexible have cancellation fees waived entirely. Medical documentation must be submitted within 14 days.

**References:** See BA12 for fee waiver thresholds, BA13 for required documentation

---

## **BA12: Medical Emergency Fee Waiver Thresholds**

Full fee waiver applies when treatment costs exceed £5,000 or hospitalization exceeds 3 consecutive days. Partial fee waiver of 50% applies when treatment costs are between £2,000-£5,000 or hospitalization is 1-2 days. No waiver applies for outpatient treatment under £2,000 unless passenger is medically unfit to fly. All thresholds require supporting medical documentation.

**References:** See BA13 for documentation requirements

---

## **BA13: Medical Emergency Documentation**

Required documents include signed letter from treating physician on official letterhead stating medical condition and unfitness to travel. Hospital admission and discharge records showing dates of treatment. Itemized medical bills or insurance claims showing treatment costs. Documentation must be in English or with certified translation. Submit via email to medicalrefunds@ba.com within 14 days of cancellation.

---

## **BA14: Economy Flexible Timing-Based Cancellation Charges**

Cancellation fees apply based on the number of days before departure when the cancellation is made. Each fee applies per passenger, per direction of travel.

- £175 per passenger for more than 15 days before departure
- £225 per passenger for 8 to 14 days before departure 
- £275 per passenger for 7 days or less before departure

The closer to departure the cancellation occurs, the higher the fee. All fees are charged per passenger and per direction (e.g., outbound and return journeys are charged separately).

**References:** See BA15 for additional route-based administrative levies

---

## **BA15: Route-Based Administrative Levies**

Long-haul routes to Americas, Asia, and Middle East add £35 administrative levy to cancellation fees. Short-haul European routes under 3 hours flight time add £15 administrative levy. Domestic UK routes add £10 administrative levy. Levies are non-waivable except for medical or bereavement cases. Total cancellation charge is timing fee plus route levy.

---

## **BA16: BA Flight Cancellations**

If British Airways cancels a flight for operational reasons, passengers receive full refund of ticket price. Refund includes all taxes, fees, and ancillary services. Alternative flights on BA or partner airlines offered at no cost. Passengers may also claim compensation under EU261 regulations if eligible. Flight delay compensation follows separate rules.

**References:** See BA17 for delay-related rules, BA20 for EU261 compensation eligibility

---

## **BA17: Flight Delays**

Delays exceeding 5 hours entitle passengers to full refund or free rebooking. Delays of 3-5 hours provide meal vouchers and accommodation if overnight. Delays under 3 hours do not qualify for compensation. Weather and air traffic control delays are considered extraordinary circumstances. Compensation claims follow EU261 procedures.

**References:** See BA20 for EU261 compensation amounts

---

## **BA18: Bereavement Eligibility & Fees**

Bereavement cancellations apply for death of immediate family member: spouse, parent, child, sibling, or grandparent. Flat £50 administrative fee applies to all fare types. Fee is waived if death occurred within 48 hours of scheduled departure. Travel must have been planned before the death occurred. Cancellation must be made within 7 days of death.

**References:** See BA19 for required documentation

---

## **BA19: Bereavement Documentation**

Required documents include official death certificate or funeral director's notice. Proof of relationship to deceased such as birth certificate, marriage certificate, or legal documents. Documentation must be submitted within 10 days of cancellation request. Submit via email to compassionatetravel@ba.com or through customer service portal.

---

## **BA20: EU261 Compensation Eligibility**

EU Regulation 261/2004 provides compensation for flight cancellations and delays departing from EU airports. Flights under 1,500km qualify for £250 per passenger. Flights 1,500-3,500km qualify for £400 per passenger. Flights over 3,500km qualify for £600 per passenger. Compensation not payable for extraordinary circumstances like severe weather, security threats, or air traffic restrictions.

---

## **BA21: Avios Redemption Bookings**

Tickets booked with Avios points follow standard cancellation policies. Cancelled tickets refund Avios points to your Executive Club account. Cash taxes and carrier charges are refundable based on fare rules. Avios refund typically processes within 7 days. Partial refunds apply if only some passengers cancel from a group booking.

---

## **BA22: Partial Journey Cancellations**

Economy Flexible allows cancellation of individual flight segments in multi-city bookings. Remaining segments remain valid for travel. Cancellation fees apply to each cancelled segment separately. Refund calculated as difference between original fare and revised journey cost. Partial cancellations must be requested at least 24 hours before affected segment departure.

**References:** See BA14 for per-segment cancellation fees, BA15 for route-specific levies

---

## **BA23: Refund Processing Timeline**

Refunds to original credit or debit cards process within 7 working days after approval. Refunds to Avios accounts process within 5 working days. Cash and check refunds require up to 20 working days. Medical and bereavement refunds may take up to 28 days pending documentation review. Partial refunds for multi-passenger bookings process separately for each traveler.

---

## **BA24: Involuntary Schedule Changes**

If British Airways changes flight schedule by more than 90 minutes, passengers may request full refund or free rebooking. Schedule changes announced at least 14 days before departure do not qualify for compensation. Last-minute changes within 14 days may qualify for EU261 compensation. Alternative routing must have comparable journey time and cabin class.

---

## **BA25: Name Corrections**

Minor spelling errors of up to 3 characters can be corrected free of charge. Full name changes to transfer ticket to different person not permitted. Name correction requests must be made at least 24 hours before departure. Legal name changes due to marriage or divorce require supporting documentation. If correction is denied, ticket must be cancelled and rebooked with cancellation fees applying.

**References:** See BA14 for applicable cancellation fees if rebooking required

---

## **BA26: Group Booking Cancellations**

Group bookings of 10 or more passengers follow specialized cancellation terms. Partial group cancellations allowed up to 10% of total passengers without penalty. Full group cancellations incur reduced fees negotiated at booking time. Medical and bereavement exceptions apply to individual passengers within groups. Group refunds process within 20 working days.

**References:** See BA11 for medical exception rules, BA18 for bereavement exception rules

---

## **BA27: Involuntary Denied Boarding**

If British Airways denies boarding due to overbooking, passengers entitled to compensation under EU261. Alternative flight must be offered within 2 hours. Meal vouchers and hotel accommodation provided if delay is overnight. Denied boarding compensation is in addition to refund or rebooking options. Voluntary denied boarding offers may provide travel vouchers instead of cash.

**References:** See BA20 for EU261 compensation amounts

---

## **BA28: Military & Emergency Travel**

Active military personnel with deployment orders may cancel tickets without penalty. Emergency travel bookings for family emergencies receive flexible change policies. Military cancellations require official military orders or government-issued travel authorization. Fee waivers do not apply to leisure or personal military travel. Emergency travel fees are similar to bereavement policies but require documentation within 5 days.

**References:** See BA18 for comparable fee structures and documentation timelines

---

## **BA30: Customer Service Contact Information**

British Airways customer service available 24/7 at +44 (0)20 8738 5050. Online refund requests submitted through ba.com account portal. Medical and bereavement cancellations email medicalrefunds@ba.com or compassionatetravel@ba.com. Live chat available on ba.com during business hours 8am-8pm GMT. Social media support via Twitter @British_Airways for urgent inquiries.

---

## **BA31: Government Travel Restrictions**

If destination country imposes entry restrictions preventing travel, passengers may rebook or receive voucher. Full cash refunds not automatic for government restrictions. Travel bans announced within 14 days of departure qualify for full refund. Passengers with existing restrictions at time of booking not eligible for refunds. Vouchers valid for 12 months from original travel date.
