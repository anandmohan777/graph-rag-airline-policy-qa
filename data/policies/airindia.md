# AIRINDIA BOOKING POLICY DOCUMENT

## **AI1: General Booking Terms**

All airindia bookings are subject to fare rules applicable at time of purchase. Tickets are valid for the specific route, date, and cabin class booked. Fare categories determine eligibility for changes, cancellations, and refunds. Infants under 2 years travel on adult's lap without separate seat allocation. All policies comply with Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA) regulations and Indian consumer protection laws.

---

## **AI2: Economy Saver Fare**

Economy Saver is the most restrictive non-refundable fare type. No refund is provided for voluntary cancellations except within 24-hour grace period. Tickets cancelled after departure forfeit all residual value. Medical emergencies and immediate family bereavement are exceptions requiring proper documentation for consideration.

**References:** See AI5 for 24-hour exceptions, AI11 for medical cancellations, AI18 for bereavement cancellations

---

## **AI3: Economy Value Fare**

Economy Value allows cancellations with applicable fees based on timing and route category. Different fee structures apply for domestic Indian routes versus international flights. Multi-city bookings permit partial segment cancellations. Fee reductions available for medical emergencies and bereavement cases with supporting documents.

**References:** See AI5 for 24-hour exceptions, AI11 for medical cancellations, AI14 for standard cancellation fees, AI18 for bereavement cancellations, AI22 for partial journey rules

---

## **AI4: Business Fare**

Business class tickets offer maximum flexibility for cancellations before departure. Administrative charge of £19 applies to voluntary cancellations for domestic flights and £48 for international flights. Refund processed to original payment method within 10 working days. Medical and bereavement cases follow same exemption criteria as Economy Value fare type.

**References:** See AI11 for medical fee waivers, AI18 for bereavement fee waivers

---

## **AI5: 24-Hour Cancellation Guarantee**

All tickets booked directly through airindia website or mobile app can be cancelled for full refund within 24 hours of purchase. Booking must be made at least 96 hours before scheduled departure for domestic flights and 7 days for international flights. Refund is automatic without additional documentation. This applies to all fare types including Economy Saver. Third-party agent bookings follow separate terms.

---

## **AI6: Special Assistance - Medical Equipment**

Passengers requiring medical equipment may bring wheelchairs, walking aids, and portable oxygen concentrators free of charge. Advance notification required at least 72 hours before departure through Special Services Request. Medical certificates must confirm equipment meets aviation safety standards and has lithium battery approval. Equipment excluded from standard baggage weight limits.

---

## **AI7: In-Flight Medical Services**

airindia aircraft carry comprehensive medical emergency kits and automated external defibrillators on all wide-body aircraft. Cabin crew receive mandatory first aid training and emergency medical response certification. Passengers with chronic medical conditions must carry prescription medication in cabin baggage. Medical emergencies may necessitate flight diversion to nearest airport with adequate facilities.

---

## **AI8: Travel Insurance - Medical Coverage**

airindia partners with insurance providers for comprehensive travel insurance covering medical emergencies, trip cancellations, and baggage loss. Insurance must be purchased before departure or within 24 hours of booking. Medical claims for trip interruption are filed directly with insurance provider. Insurance coverage supplements but does not replace airindia cancellation policies.

---

## **AI9: Flight Changes & Amendment Fees**

Flight changes permitted based on fare type and ticket restrictions. Change fees range from £24 to £57 depending on route type and advance notice period. Fare difference applies when new flight costs more than original booking. Changes within 24 hours of departure incur same-day modification fees. Name corrections follow different procedures.

**References:** See AI25 for name correction policies

---

## **AI10: Compassionate Travel - Bereavement Booking Discounts**

airindia provides discounted bereavement fares for immediate family deaths requiring urgent travel. Discount applies to new bookings only and cannot be applied retroactively. Death certificate or hospital certificate required within 72 hours of booking. Bereavement fares are non-refundable but permit one free date change within validity period.

---

## **AI11: Medical Emergency Eligibility & Refund Terms**

Medical cancellations require passenger or immediate family member hospitalization or serious illness diagnosis from registered medical practitioner. Refund amount varies by original fare type. Economy Saver receives full refund minus £14 processing fee. Economy Value and Business fares have cancellation fees waived completely. Medical documentation must be submitted within 21 days of cancellation.

**References:** See AI12 for fee waiver thresholds, AI13 for required documentation

---

## **AI12: Medical Emergency Fee Waiver Thresholds**

Full fee waiver applies when hospitalization exceeds 48 consecutive hours or treatment costs exceed £715. Partial fee waiver of 50% applies when hospitalization is 24-48 hours or treatment costs between £285-£715. No waiver for outpatient treatment under £285 unless medical certificate states passenger unfit to fly. All thresholds require authenticated medical documentation.

**References:** See AI13 for documentation requirements

---

## **AI13: Medical Emergency Documentation**

Required documents include signed medical certificate from registered medical practitioner on official hospital letterhead stating diagnosis and travel fitness assessment. Hospital admission and discharge summary showing treatment dates. Medical bills or insurance claim documents showing itemized treatment costs. Documents in regional languages require English translation with notarization. Submit via email to medicalrefunds.airindia@airindia.in within 21 days of cancellation.

---

## **AI14: Economy Value Timing-Based Cancellation Charges**

Cancellation fees apply based on days before departure when cancellation is requested. Fees charged per passenger per flight segment.

- £29 per passenger for more than 15 days before departure
- £38 per passenger for 8 to 14 days before departure
- £48 per passenger for 4 to 7 days before departure
- £57 per passenger for less than 4 days before departure

Earlier cancellations incur lower fees. Each flight segment charged separately for multi-city bookings.

**References:** See AI15 for additional route-based charges

---

## **AI15: Route-Based Administrative Levies**

International flights to Americas, Europe, and Australia add £14 administrative levy to cancellation fees. Gulf and Southeast Asia routes add £10 administrative levy. Domestic routes add £5 administrative levy. SAARC region flights add £7 administrative levy. Levies are non-waivable except for medical or bereavement cases. Total cancellation charge is timing fee plus route levy.

---

## **AI16: AirIndia Flight Cancellations**

When airindia cancels flight for operational or technical reasons, passengers receive full refund including ticket price, taxes, and all fees. Alternative flights on airindia or partner airlines offered at no additional cost. Passengers may rebook on next available flight or choose full refund. Compensation follows DGCA regulations for denied boarding and cancellations. Flight delay compensation has separate provisions.

**References:** See AI17 for delay-related rules, AI20 for DGCA compensation eligibility

---

## **AI17: Flight Delays**

Delays exceeding 4 hours entitle passengers to full refund or free rebooking. Delays of 2-4 hours provide meal vouchers and hotel accommodation if required overnight stay. Delays under 2 hours do not qualify for compensation. Weather disruptions and air traffic control delays considered extraordinary circumstances. Compensation claims follow DGCA Civil Aviation Requirements.

**References:** See AI20 for DGCA compensation amounts

---

## **AI18: Bereavement Eligibility & Fees**

Bereavement cancellations apply for death of immediate family member: spouse, parent, child, sibling, grandparent, or parent-in-law. Flat £10 administrative fee applies to all fare types for domestic flights and £24 for international flights. Fee waived if death occurred within 72 hours of scheduled departure. Travel must have been booked before death occurred. Cancellation must be requested within 10 days of death.

**References:** See AI19 for required documentation

---

## **AI19: Bereavement Documentation**

Required documents include original or certified copy of death certificate issued by municipal authority. Proof of relationship through birth certificate, marriage certificate, or family register. Documentation must be submitted within 14 days of cancellation request. Submit via email to compassionatetravel.airindia@airindia.in or through customer service portal with booking reference.

---

## **AI20: DGCA Compensation Eligibility**

DGCA Civil Aviation Requirements provide compensation for flight cancellations and extended delays on domestic Indian flights. Delays over 4 hours on domestic flights qualify for £48 per passenger. International flights follow destination country regulations. Compensation not applicable for extraordinary circumstances including severe weather, security threats, air traffic restrictions, or airport operational issues.

---

## **AI21: Flying Returns Redemption Bookings**

Tickets booked using Flying Returns frequent flyer miles follow standard cancellation policies. Cancelled tickets refund miles to Flying Returns account. Cash taxes and fuel surcharges refundable based on fare rules. Miles refund typically processes within 10 working days. Partial refunds apply when some passengers cancel from group booking while others travel.

---

## **AI22: Partial Journey Cancellations**

Economy Value permits cancellation of individual flight segments in multi-city itineraries. Remaining segments stay valid for travel as per original booking. Cancellation fees apply separately to each cancelled segment. Refund calculated as difference between original total fare and cost of revised journey. Partial cancellations must be requested minimum 48 hours before affected segment departure.

**References:** See AI14 for per-segment cancellation fees, AI15 for route-specific levies

---

## **AI23: Refund Processing Timeline**

Refunds to original credit or debit cards process within 10 working days after approval. Refunds to Flying Returns accounts process within 7 working days. Net banking and UPI refunds take up to 15 working days. Medical and bereavement refunds may require up to 30 working days pending documentation verification. Multi-passenger bookings process each refund separately based on individual passenger status.

---

## **AI24: Involuntary Schedule Changes**

When airindia changes flight schedule by more than 2 hours, passengers may request full refund or free alternative flight. Schedule changes announced minimum 15 days before departure do not qualify for additional compensation. Last-minute changes within 15 days may qualify for DGCA compensation. Alternative routing must maintain comparable journey duration and cabin class.

---

## **AI25: Name Corrections**

Minor spelling corrections up to 3 characters can be made free of charge if requested more than 24 hours before departure. Complete name changes to transfer ticket to different person are not permitted. Name correction requests must be made minimum 48 hours before departure through customer service. Legal name changes from marriage or divorce require supporting government documents. If correction denied, ticket must be cancelled and rebooked with applicable cancellation fees.

**References:** See AI14 for applicable cancellation fees if rebooking required

---

## **AI26: Group Booking Cancellations**

Group bookings of 10 or more passengers have specialized cancellation terms negotiated at booking time. Partial group cancellations permitted up to 15% of total passengers without penalty if requested 30 days before departure. Full group cancellations incur graduated fees based on advance notice period. Medical and bereavement exceptions apply to individual passengers within groups. Group refunds process within 30 working days.

**References:** See AI11 for medical exception rules, AI18 for bereavement exception rules

---

## **AI27: Involuntary Denied Boarding**

When airindia denies boarding due to overselling, passengers entitled to compensation per DGCA regulations. Alternative flight offered within 4 hours or full refund provided. Meal vouchers and hotel accommodation provided for overnight delays. Denied boarding compensation is additional to refund or rebooking options. Voluntary denied boarding offers may provide upgrade vouchers or travel credits instead of cash.

**References:** See AI20 for DGCA compensation amounts

---

## **AI28: Military & Emergency Travel**

Active Indian Armed Forces personnel with official deployment orders may cancel tickets without penalty. Emergency travel bookings for urgent family matters receive flexible change options. Military cancellations require official movement orders or government-issued authorization. Fee waivers apply only to official duty travel, not personal leisure travel. Emergency travel fees follow similar structure to bereavement policies with documentation required within 7 days.

**References:** See AI18 for comparable fee structures and documentation timelines

## **AI31: Government Travel Restrictions**

When destination country imposes entry restrictions preventing passenger travel, rebooking or travel voucher provided. Full cash refunds not automatic for government-imposed restrictions. Travel bans announced within 14 days of departure qualify for full refund. Passengers aware of restrictions at time of booking not eligible for refunds. Vouchers valid for 12 months from original travel date and transferable once.
